National Art School Postgraduate Exhibition 2025

The 2025 National Art School Postgraduate Exhibition was presented across two sites. Each graduating artist exhibited work in both the NAS Gallery and their individual studio, allowing audiences to encounter finished works in a curated exhibition setting as well as the ongoing context of each artist's practice.
